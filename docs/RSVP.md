# RSVPs

Events can take RSVPs for each date. Turn them on from the event form, and set a capacity if the date should fill up. Leave capacity blank for an unlimited event. A single date can override the capacity or turn RSVPs off.

Public events accept a name and email from people without an account. The confirmation email links to a page that works until the event is over. If that email already belongs to an account, the message tells them to log in. Members-only and private events require a login.

When a capacity-limited date is full, new RSVPs join the wait list. The host chooses what happens when a spot opens:

- **First come, first served** (the default). Everyone waiting is emailed, and the first to claim the spot gets it.
- **One person at a time.** The spot is held for the next person. If they don't accept in time, it is offered to the next person. They stay on the wait list either way. In the last few hours the date switches to first come, first served.

Hosts and admins can open the list from the date page. They can cancel someone, promote someone from the wait list, add a person, resend the email, and check people in during the event. Hosts see names only. For members, the name shown is their username. Admins also see the full name and email. The CSV follows the same rule.

Each host can turn on "Email me about RSVPs" on the event page. That sends one email per new RSVP, cancellation, or wait-list join.

Confirmed people get a reminder a week before, a day before, and on the morning of the event. The day-before reminder asks them to cancel if they can't make it when the date is full or has a wait list. They can opt out of reminders from the link in the email.

Guest names and emails are cleared 30 days after the event. That period, the offer window, and the site-wide RSVP switch are in site settings.
